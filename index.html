About

"Joining the dots across regulation, risk, technology and commercial strategy."

Amarjit Singh is a Partner at EY with over seventeen years at the firm, leading the UK&I Digital Assets practice and serving as EMEIA Blockchain Assurance Leader. He sits at the intersection of emerging technology, financial regulation and institutional governance — advising FTSE-listed boards, asset managers and government bodies on navigating rapidly evolving digital landscapes.

A Cambridge-trained computer scientist who crossed into financial services, Amarjit has spent his career translating complexity into clarity. He has worked across fund tokenisation, crypto asset custody, AI governance, and the regulatory treatment of distributed ledger technology in wholesale markets.

Beyond EY, he chairs ICAEW's Digital Assets SteerCo and serves as Audit & Risk Committee Chair for NHS Charities Together — bringing the same rigour he applies in boardrooms to institutions that matter beyond the bottom line.
